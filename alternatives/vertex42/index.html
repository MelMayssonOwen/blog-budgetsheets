Vertex42 alternatives in 2026: other free and paid budget spreadsheets

Updated August 2026

Vertex42 is the free incumbent for spreadsheet budgeting (its Money Management Template lists “Over 750,000 downloads”). If you want an alternative, the honest options are: the built-in budget templates in Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel (also free); a one-time-purchase template with a more modern design ($9, $29); or Tiller ($99/year) if you want the same spreadsheet approach but with automatic bank sync. Most people switch away from Vertex42 for design taste or for automation, not because it lacks budgeting capability.

Why people switchReasons are almost always design (Vertex42’s layouts are dense) or automation (it has none). If neither bothers you, there’s little reason to move off a free, proven template.

Frequently asked questions

What's a good free alternative to Vertex42?
The built-in budget templates in Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel are both free and often more modern-looking. Google Sheets adds easy sharing and a mobile app. All three, Vertex42, Google, Excel, do the same manual budgeting.
Is there an alternative to Vertex42 with bank sync?
Yes, Tiller ($99/year). It's the spreadsheet-based option that adds automatic transaction import, which Vertex42 doesn't have. Everything else stays a manual spreadsheet.
Should I pay for a template instead of using Vertex42?
Only if a better-designed, ready-made layout would actually keep you budgeting. A one-time template ($9, $29) buys polish and time saved, not extra capability. Vertex42 already covers the budgeting math for free.
